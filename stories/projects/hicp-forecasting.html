Time series

Forecasting the Harmonised Index of Consumer Prices

Université Nazi Boni · November to December 2021

Inflation has a trend and a season. Which forecasting family handles it best?

01Context

Monthly HICP data, a real economic series with trend and seasonality.

02Question

Which family of classical methods suits this series, and why?

03Obstacle

Smoothing, trend and autoregressive models make different assumptions; fitting one model in isolation would not show which assumptions hold.

04Choices

Which methods to compare?methodological question

Tension
Each family captures different structures.
Choice
Simple and double exponential smoothing, Holt-Winters, trend regression, ARIMA and SARIMA on the same series.
05Work
  1. Monthly HICP
  2. Exploration (trend, season)
  3. Smoothing models
  4. Trend regression
  5. ARIMA / SARIMA
  6. Comparison
06Checks
  • All methods compared on the same series with the same workflow.
07Outcome

A structured comparison showing how smoothing, trend, autoregressive and seasonal approaches behave on the same data, and a stronger model-selection practice.

08Limits

What remains open

  • Classical methods only; no comparison with machine-learning forecasters.

What I took from it

  • Compare model families on the same footing before choosing one.

The whole project in one sentenceComparing classical forecasting families on the same HICP series showed which structures the data really needs (trend, seasonality, autocorrelation) before choosing a model.
