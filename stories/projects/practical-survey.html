Survey methodology

A complete survey, from protocol to dataset

Université Nazi Boni · May to June 2023

Most analyses start from a dataset. This project started from an empty page.

01Context

A practical survey project covering the whole chain, from defining the problem to collecting data in the field.

02Question

How to design a survey so that the data collected actually answers the question asked?

03Obstacle

Each step constrains the next: the sample size depends on the objectives, the questionnaire on the analysis planned, and data quality on the collection tools.

04Choices

How many people to survey?methodological question

Tension
Too few gives imprecise results, too many costs time.
Choice
A sample-size calculation derived from the objectives.

How to collect reliable data in the field?methodological question

Tension
Paper forms produce entry errors.
Choice
A structured digital form built with KoboCollect.
05Work
  1. Objectives and hypotheses
  2. Protocol
  3. Sample size
  4. Questionnaire and coding
  5. KoboCollect form
  6. Field collection
  7. Analysis dataset
06Checks
  • Consistency between questionnaire, coding and planned analysis.
  • Data-quality checks after collection.
07Outcome

An operational survey workflow and an analysis-ready dataset, with hands-on experience of field data quality.

08Limits

What remains open

  • A training-scale survey, not a large representative study.

What I took from it

  • Good analysis starts with good collection design.

The whole project in one sentenceFrom objectives to a clean dataset, each step was designed for the one after it, which is what makes field data usable for analysis.
