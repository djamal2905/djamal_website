The link between gaming and adolescent health

ENSAI · December 2024 to May 2025

Are gaming and gambling habits linked to how teenagers feel, sleep and rate their health?

01Context

ESCAPAD 2022 survey data on adolescents, with gaming and gambling habits and physical and mental health indicators.

02Question

Which health indicators are associated with intensive gaming, and do profiles differ between boys and girls?

03Obstacle

Survey data show associations, not causes. Many categorical variables had to be summarised without over-interpreting them.

04Choices

How to test associations between categorical variables?methodological question

Tension
Some categories are small.
Choice
Chi-square tests, and Fisher exact tests where counts were too small.

How to see profiles across many variables at once?methodological question

Tension
Pairwise tests miss the overall structure.
Choice
Multiple Correspondence Analysis (MCA) to map profiles.
05Work
  1. Survey data
  2. Cleaning and recoding
  3. Exploratory analysis
  4. Chi-square and Fisher tests
  5. MCA profiles
06Checks
  • Tests adapted to small counts.
  • Careful wording: associations, not effects.
07Outcome

Intensive gaming was associated with less favourable indicators, such as a higher ADRS depression score, sleep problems and worse self-perceived health, with distinct profiles by gender.

08Limits

What remains open

  • Cross-sectional survey: no causal conclusion is possible.

What I took from it

  • Statistical caution in the wording is part of the result.

The whole project in one sentenceIn ESCAPAD 2022, intensive gaming goes with worse depression scores, sleep and self-rated health, with different profiles for boys and girls: associations worth monitoring, not proof that gaming causes them.
