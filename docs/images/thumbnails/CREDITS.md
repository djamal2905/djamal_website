## Why these seven

The pre-existing thumbnails for these seven cards were either:
- AI-generated placeholder images with garbled/nonsensical text baked into
  them (`tumors_classi.png`, `gestes.png`, `fraude.png`, `clustering.png`,
  `medical_classification.png` — all under the archived
  `content/archive/publications-legacy/stat_ml/images/` path, which
  `personal-projects.qmd` was still pointing to via the old, now-broken
  `publications/stat_ml/images/...` path), or
- generic abstract vector illustrations that didn't depict the project's
  real-world subject matter (`images/academic/nba-career.png`,
  `images/blog/sig-carto.png`).

Every other thumbnail on the site (real app screenshots, the existing
abstract illustrations for the remaining academic-project cards, the
certification images, etc.) was left as-is — this pass only replaced images
that were either broken or clearly asked for by name in the brief.

## License notes

- **Public domain** files (U.S. federal government works, or explicitly
  CC0/PD-dedicated) carry no legal attribution requirement, but the
  photographer/agency is still noted above for reference.
- **CC BY 2.0** (`anomaly-detection-gmm.jpg`) technically requires
  attribution; it's recorded here and the credit can be surfaced on-page if
  ever required.
- **Pexels License** photos are free for commercial and personal use with
  no attribution required, per Pexels' own license terms.

No image from an unlicensed reverse-image-search result was used; every file
above traces back to Wikimedia Commons or Pexels with a verifiable license
tag on its source page.
