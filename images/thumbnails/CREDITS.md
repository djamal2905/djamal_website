## Why these images

The pre-existing thumbnails for these cards were either:
- AI-generated placeholder images with garbled/nonsensical text baked into
  them (the original five `publications/stat_ml/images/*.png` references in
  personal-projects.qmd — see below), or
- generic abstract vector illustrations that didn't depict the project's or
  article's real-world subject matter (all six `images/academic/*.png` and
  all six `images/blog/*.png` references, plus `images/academic/nba-career.png`
  and `images/blog/sig-carto.png` replaced in an earlier pass).

Every project/article's actual description (and, for academic-projects.qmd,
its modal case-study text) was read first so each replacement genuinely
matches the work — e.g. FuelTrack is a fuel-price app (gas pump, not a
generic chart), HICP forecasting is inflation/economic time series (a
trending price chart), nlp-scibert mines biomedical abstracts (a doctor
reading medical literature, not a lab-bench photo).

Real screenshots, certification images, and the SocialNetworkAnalysis.png
choice above (a genuine network-analysis graph, not a stock photo) were
prioritized over generic stock imagery wherever a subject allowed it.

### Earlier pass (personal-projects.qmd)

- `tumors_classi.png`, `gestes.png`, `fraude.png`, `clustering.png`,
  `medical_classification.png` — AI-generated, archived under
  `content/archive/publications-legacy/stat_ml/images/`; personal-projects.qmd
  was still pointing at the pre-reorg `publications/stat_ml/images/...` path,
  which had gone 404.

### Later pass: two real screenshots swapped anyway

`voice-assistant.jpg` and `java-mysql-desktop-app.jpg` replaced two images
that were technically real (not AI-generated or generic stock art) but that
the owner still wanted changed. `content/programming/voice-assistant/` and
`content/programming/java-mysql-desktop-app/` were checked first for any
better unused screenshot: the voice-assistant folder only had its video file
and the same abstract placeholder graphic already in use, and the desktop
app folder's one unused image showed the developer's own Windows desktop
icons rather than the application itself, so neither folder had a usable
alternative and both were replaced with sourced photography instead.

  CC0/PD-dedicated) carry no legal attribution requirement, but the
  photographer/agency is still noted above for reference.
- **CC BY 2.0 / CC BY-SA 4.0** (`anomaly-detection-gmm.jpg`,
  `network-diffusion.png`) technically require attribution; it's recorded

  attribution to the National Cancer Institute, recorded above.

  no attribution required, per Pexels' own license terms.

No image from an unlicensed reverse-image-search result was used; every file
above traces back to Wikimedia Commons or Pexels with a verifiable license
tag on its source page.
